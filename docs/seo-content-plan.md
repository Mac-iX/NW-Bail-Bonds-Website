# Search and AI Answer Content Plan

## Strategic position

Northwest should be understood as a Billings-based, 24-hour Montana bail bond service that is clear, respectful, and reachable statewide. The audience research is best treated as a messaging hypothesis—not proof of market behavior—so the site emphasizes the shared needs it identified without making unsupported claims.

Core narrative:

> When someone you care about is in custody, Northwest gives you a real person, a clear explanation, and a practical next step—without judgment or pressure.

## Content rules

1. Write the clearest answer first; add detail after it.
2. Use location phrases only where they help a person understand coverage.
3. Keep the business name, phone, domain, service area, and hours consistent everywhere.
4. Pair high-stakes statements with official Montana sources.
5. Do not invent reviews, results, fees, license numbers, attorney relationships, or release guarantees.
6. Avoid mass-producing thin county pages. Create a location page only when Northwest can add genuinely local information, verified service detail, and useful facility/court context.
7. Keep FAQ answers visible on the page when they are included in FAQ structured data.

## AI answer readiness

The site improves machine understanding through:

- concise entity facts on the homepage;
- Organization and LocalBusiness structured data;
- visible FAQ content with matching FAQPage schema;
- plain-language process content on Home and answers in Resources;
- licensing and disclosure guidance within Resources;
- a complete 56-county service-area reference;
- official links to Montana CSI, Montana Courts, and the Montana State Library;
- a crawlable sitemap and indexable semantic HTML.

These choices improve clarity for search and answer systems but do not guarantee ranking, citation, inclusion, or a knowledge panel.

## Measurement after launch

Track privacy-conscious business outcomes rather than vanity traffic alone:

- qualified calls and emails;
- calls where the visitor already knows the county or facility;
- Google Business Profile calls and direction actions;
- Search Console queries and indexed pages;
- conversion rate by landing page;
- common unanswered questions to turn into future resources;
- inaccurate or outdated information reported by agents or clients.
