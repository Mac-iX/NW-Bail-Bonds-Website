# Launch Content Checklist

The site is intentionally conservative where client facts have not been verified. Complete this checklist before making the final domain public and requesting indexing.

## Business identity

- [ ] Confirm the exact public brand: `Northwest Bail Bonds`, `Northwest Bail Bondsman`, or another legal DBA.
- [ ] Confirm the legal business entity name.
- [ ] Confirm the preferred domain and account ownership.
- [ ] Confirm the physical or service-area address that may be published.
- [ ] Confirm the primary phone remains `(406) 601-1225`.
- [ ] Add the client-controlled email address if it should be public.

Use the same name, phone, URL, and address format on the website, Google Business Profile, Facebook, Montana licensing records, and major directories.

## Licensing and credibility

- [ ] Obtain the current individual producer name and license details.
- [ ] Obtain the current agency or business entity license details.
- [ ] Verify both through the Montana Commissioner of Securities and Insurance.
- [ ] Confirm any surety or carrier language the client is authorized to publish.
- [ ] Confirm any attorney relationship in writing before mentioning it.
- [ ] Add current credentials to `/licensing` and the home-page credibility section.

## Pricing and service terms

- [ ] Confirm how the client may describe fees under current Montana rules and contracts.
- [ ] Confirm whether collateral is sometimes required and how it may be described.
- [ ] Confirm whether payment plans exist and their qualifying language.
- [ ] Confirm accepted payment methods.
- [ ] Confirm refund, cancellation, or collateral-return language.

Do not publish “lowest price,” “guaranteed approval,” “no collateral,” “instant release,” or a specific payment plan without written client verification.

## Real people and community proof

- [ ] Photograph the owners and agents in natural Montana settings and a clean indoor location.
- [ ] Collect each team member's preferred name, role, short bio, and written approval.
- [ ] Document community programs, events, charities, or educational work before claiming involvement.
- [ ] Gather testimonials only with written client permission and a privacy-safe display name.
- [ ] Never use case details, arrest information, or identifying facts in a testimonial without explicit written consent and legal review.

## Accounts and links

- [ ] Confirm the official Facebook page URL and admin access.
- [ ] Create or verify the Google Business Profile.
- [ ] Create Google Search Console and submit `/sitemap.xml` after launch.
- [ ] Create a client-owned analytics account only if desired; update the privacy notice first.
- [ ] Confirm domain registrar access and add the final DNS records.

## Final launch QA

- [ ] Set `NEXT_PUBLIC_SITE_URL` to the final canonical domain.
- [ ] Test phone links on iPhone and Android.
- [ ] Test the prepared email workflow on iPhone and Android.
- [ ] Review every page with the client for factual accuracy.
- [ ] Test at 360px, 768px, 1024px, and desktop widths.
- [ ] Check keyboard navigation, focus visibility, contrast, and reduced-motion behavior.
- [ ] Validate Organization and FAQ structured data.
- [ ] Confirm `/robots.txt` and `/sitemap.xml` use the final domain.
- [ ] Verify that no sensitive information is collected by an unintended backend.
